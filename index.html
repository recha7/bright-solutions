Policies

Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to serve you, such as your name, WhatsApp number and the files you send for your order. We use it only to complete your work and contact you about it. We never sell or share your information or documents with others. You can ask us to delete your files after your order is complete. If you post feedback on this site, the name, location, comment, photos and time you submit are shown publicly; ask us on WhatsApp and we will remove them.

Terms of service

Prices and delivery times are agreed on WhatsApp before work starts. Work begins once payment, or an agreed deposit, is confirmed. Please check your finished work and tell us about corrections within 24 hours. Data bundles and deals are delivered once payment is confirmed.

Refund policy

If we make a mistake, we will fix it free of charge. If we cannot deliver what we agreed, you get a refund for the undelivered part. Completed data bundle purchases cannot be reversed. Contact us on WhatsApp to raise any issue.

File confidentiality

Documents and designs you send us stay private. We use them only for your order.
